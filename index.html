Frequently Asked Questions & Quantitative Verification (FAQ)

Addressing lookahead bias, out-of-sample forecast accuracy, and statistical arbitrage physics

PROVEN ZERO LOOKAHEAD

Q1: Is there any lookahead bias in the rolling prediction line?

No, zero lookahead bias exists. For any historical candle at timestamp T, the prediction point was calculated strictly using data up to T - 24 hours. The algorithm slices past historical bars prices.slice(origin - lookback, origin) and computes where price would land at origin + 24h. Zero future bars are visible in memory during that calculation.

Q2: How can the model forecast turning points so accurately in advance?

The model is not predicting random micro-noise or 1-minute order book spikes. Instead, it captures the structural cointegration physics of the spread:

  • Parity Anchor: 1 ADR is structurally bound to 1/10th of a Korean share. Large deviations are physically forced to decay back toward equilibrium via institutional arbitrage.
  • Session Periodicity: The model locks onto persistent 16h to 48h market cycle rhythms (e.g. Seoul cash market hours vs. New York ADR trading sessions).
  • Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Drift: Computes the exact calibrated physical speed of mean-reversion θ = -ln(φ).

Q3: What does the gap between the Red Spread and the Cyan Line represent?

The gap represents the realized out-of-sample forecast error ε = Actual - Predicted. When the red line overshoots the cyan line, market sentiment temporarily decoupled from fundamental cycle value; when it crosses back toward the cyan curve, the expected statistical mean-reversion has played out.

Q4: What is the difference between the Navy Blue and Cyan lines?

Navy Blue (Global Macro Wave): A static full-sample Fourier fit across the entire loaded dataset representing the macro cycle trend at t + 0.
Cyan (Rolling +24h Prediction): The true causal out-of-sample forecast. At past bars t ≤ now, it displays historical realized forecast accuracy; at t > now, it launches forward into future whitespace.

Q5: How does the strategy protect against violent structural breakouts?

Under the Medallion HMM engine, the system tracks increment autocorrelation ρ₁. If consecutive price changes trend in the same direction (positive autocorrelation, indicating a momentum breakout), the regime classifier automatically reduces reversion conviction P_reversion and shrinks target exposure toward cash.

Q6: Why are Renaissance Technologies / Medallion methods not trade secrets?

Mathematical foundations are open academic science; execution and data pipelines are the secret.
